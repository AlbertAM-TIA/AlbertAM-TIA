Agricultural systems scientist with research experience in the UK, Australia and New Zealand. I integrate crop and livestock modelling with farm economics to assess climate adaptation, soil carbon and greenhouse-gas emissions. My work combines APSIM, SGS and OpenSimRoot with R/Python, explainable AI and reproducible analysis. I co-developed and evaluated WaterCan Profit and led modelling and open-data outputs for Cranfield University’s FixOurFood programme. I develop open research tools and interactive dashboards to connect scientific evidence with agricultural decisions.
